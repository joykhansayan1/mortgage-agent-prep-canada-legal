Purchases

I paid and the app still shows the purchase screen

Open the purchase screen or the app's Settings screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase — a purchase is tied to that account. If it still does not unlock, check that you have a network connection, force-quit and reopen the app, then email support with the date of purchase.

Where is Restore Purchases after I have bought?

It is in Settings inside the app, under Subscription. Reach Settings by tapping the gear icon at the top right of the Home tab. It is available at all times, before and after purchase.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

In the app: Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription. On the device: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not shorten the period you have already paid for, and access continues to the end of that period. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

Do I need a subscription, or is Lifetime enough?

All three plans unlock exactly the same thing — the whole app, all three provinces. Monthly and Yearly are auto-renewable subscriptions. Lifetime Full Access is a one-time purchase and does not renew. If you expect to be studying for more than about four months, Lifetime is usually the cheaper choice.

Is there a free trial or a free version?

No. The app is a hard paywall with no free tier and no free sample content, so that the whole bank can be bundled offline with no advertising and no data collection.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled entirely by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com or through Apple Support. The developer cannot issue, approve or refuse a refund.

Can I use it on my iPhone and my iPad?

Yes. One purchase covers both, as long as both devices use the same Apple Account. If the second device shows the purchase screen, tap Restore Purchases.

Using the app

How do I change province?

Tap the province banner at the top of the Home tab, or go to Settings → Province. Changing province re-filters the question bank, the timed exam format and the pass mark. It does not delete your progress — your per-question history stays on the device, so switching back restores it.

Why do I see fewer questions than the total?

The bank holds 2,502 questions. Federal rules and shared concepts apply in all three provinces; anything that differs between them is tagged to the provinces it is actually correct for. So a single province sees roughly 2,100, and you are never shown another province's rule as if it were your own. The Home tab shows the exact count for your province.

What is the "Rules Drill"?

It is only the items that state a rule specific to your province — the provincial statute, the licence classes, the transfer tax, the enforcement route, the errors and omissions requirement. It is the fastest way to separate provincial rules from federal ones in your head.

Why does the app qualify at a higher rate than the contract rate?

Because the examinations do. The minimum qualifying rate is the greater of the contract rate plus two percentage points or 5.25%, and debt service ratios are computed at that rate even though the borrower pays the contract rate. The ratio trainer shows both figures side by side so the distinction becomes automatic.

Why are the dollar amounts in Canadian dollars when the price is in US dollars?

The rules being tested — insured down-payment tiers, the C$1,500,000 insured property ceiling, errors and omissions minimums, land transfer tax brackets — are Canadian statutory and programme amounts, so restating them in another currency would make the study content factually wrong. App pricing is separate and is shown in US dollars; the App Store charges in your local currency.

Where do the topic percentages come from?

They are this app's own study weightings. None of FSRA, BCFSA or RECA publishes a topic weighting table for its mortgage examinations, so the app allocates weight according to how much of the published curriculum and legislation each area occupies, and says so wherever the percentages appear.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. The entire bank ships inside the app. A network connection is only needed to buy or restore a purchase, or to open a citation link you tap.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

Settings → Study Reminder. It is one local notification a day at a time you choose, scheduled on your device. If you previously declined the iOS notification permission, the app shows a link to iOS Settings so you can change it there.

How does spaced repetition decide what to show me?

Each question sits in one of five review boxes. Answer it correctly and it moves up a box and comes back later (1, 2, 4, 8 then 16 days). Get it wrong and it drops to box one and returns tomorrow. Daily Review on the Home tab is whatever is due today.

Can I reset everything and start again?

Settings → Study Data → Reset Progress, or the button at the bottom of the Progress tab. It deletes your statistics, streaks, bookmarks, exam history and ratio-drill results. It cannot be undone.

Is my practice score a prediction of my real result?

No, and the app says so on every screen that shows a score. The readiness percentage measures your performance inside this app only. The app's study benchmark is deliberately set above your province's published pass mark so your practice carries a margin.

Does this count as my pre-licensing course?

No. It is independent study material and is not an approved or accredited course. It does not satisfy any education requirement. You still have to complete the programme your regulator approves.

Reporting a content error

Legislation, premium rates and programme rules change, and the developer wants to know about anything that has gone stale or reads wrongly. Email dmjks120@gmail.com with:

  • the subtopic label shown above the rationale (for example Debt service · gross debt service ratio);
  • enough of the question wording to find it;
  • the province you have selected;
  • what you believe the correct position is, and the source you are relying on.

Corrections are made in the next update and are genuinely appreciated.

Accessibility

The app supports Dynamic Type at every size, VoiceOver labels on every control, Dark Mode, reduced motion, and a read-aloud button on every question and rationale. If something is hard to read or hard to reach with VoiceOver, email support and describe where — it will be fixed.
