What this app is

Mortgage Agent Prep Canada is an independent iOS study app for people working towards a provincial mortgage licence: the Mortgage Agent Level 1 licence in Ontario, the submortgage broker registration (becoming the Mortgage Broker licence under the Mortgage Services Act) in British Columbia, and the Mortgage Associate licence in Alberta.

You pick your province once. From that point the question bank, the timed exam format, the pass mark and the licensing content all follow that choice, so you never revise another province's rule as if it were your own.

A purchase is required to use the app. There is no free tier and no free sample content.

What is inside

2,502 original questions

Every question written for this app, each with a full rationale and a named Canadian primary source you can tap through to. Around 2,100 apply to any single province.

420 worked calculations

Gross and total debt service ratios, monthly and accelerated payments, loan-to-value, minimum down payment, insurance premiums, three months' interest, interest rate differential and provincial transfer taxes — each solved line by line rather than just answered.

GDS / TDS ratio trainer

Build a ratio from a borrower's figures and watch each line assemble, work backwards from an income to a maximum mortgage, or drill randomly generated ratio and payment questions against the clock.

Province-tagged rules

Ontario, British Columbia and Alberta rules are tagged separately from the federal ones, and the province-specific items can be drilled on their own.

Timed to your province

Full-length, half-length and short timed sets at the question count, time limit and pass mark published for the jurisdiction you are licensing in.

Spaced repetition

A Leitner queue brings each question back before you forget it, plus flashcards, bookmarks, an incorrect-answer review and weak-area drills.

Readiness analytics

Weighted readiness, per-topic mastery, a score trend, an exam countdown, a daily goal and a study streak — all computed on the device.

Offline and private

No account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and nothing sent off your device. The whole bank is bundled in the app.

Original content, cited sources

Every question, rationale, worked calculation, scenario and study outline is original writing produced for this app. Nothing is copied from any course provider, textbook, regulator publication or other app, and no official examination question is reproduced.

Rules and figures are written from publicly published Canadian primary sources, and each rationale names the one it came from. Those sources include the Mortgage Brokerages, Lenders and Administrators Act, 2006 and O. Regs. 409/07, 188/08, 189/08 and 191/08; the BC Mortgage Brokers Act, Mortgage Services Act and Business Practices and Consumer Protection Act; the Alberta Real Estate Act and Real Estate Act Rules; the Interest Act; OSFI Guideline B-20; Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation programme rules; the federal Cost of Borrowing regulations; FINTRAC's mortgage sector guidance; the Financial Consumer Agency of Canada; and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Limits, premium rates and programme rules change. The app tells you to confirm the current figure with the primary source before relying on it, and every source is one tap away inside the app.
