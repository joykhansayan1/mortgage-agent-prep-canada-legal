The short version

Mortgage Agent Prep Canada does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no user profile and no server operated by the developer. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no tracking pixel and no third-party software development kit of any kind. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not track you across apps or websites, and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission because it has nothing to track.

Who is responsible

This app is published by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy, an individual developer. Questions about this policy can be sent to dmjks120@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

The app writes the following to your own device only, using Apple's standard on-device storage (SwiftData and UserDefaults). None of it is transmitted anywhere.

WhatWhyWhere
Your selected province (Ontario, British Columbia or Alberta) To filter the question bank and the exam format to your jurisdiction On your device
Per-question statistics: times seen, times correct, last result, spaced-repetition box and next review date, bookmark flag To power review, spaced repetition, mastery and weak-area drills On your device
Timed-set results: date, score, duration, per-topic breakdown, province and pass mark To show your score history and trend On your device
Ratio-trainer drill results and the figures you type into the trainer To show drill history and to keep your inputs between sessions On your device
Daily activity counts To compute your daily goal progress and study streak On your device
Onboarding answers: your target exam date, goal, focus topics and daily goal To build your study plan and countdown On your device
Reminder preference: whether a daily reminder is on and at what time To schedule one optional local notification On your device

You can erase all of it at any time with Reset Progress in Settings or on the Progress tab. Deleting the app also deletes it.

Figures you type into the ratio trainer

The debt service ratio trainer lets you enter figures such as an income, a mortgage amount, a property tax bill and monthly debt payments. These are stored on your device so your inputs survive closing the app. They are never transmitted, never seen by the developer, and are not used for anything other than showing you the calculation. They are not a mortgage application and are not sent to any lender.

Permissions

The app requests exactly one permission, and only if you ask for it:

  • Notifications — requested only if you switch on the optional daily study reminder. The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device by iOS; nothing is sent through any server. If you leave the reminder off, the app never asks. If you decline, the app simply turns the preference back off and points you at iOS Settings; it does not ask again.

The app does not request or use the camera, the microphone, the photo library, contacts, location, calendars, reminders, health data, Bluetooth, local network access, motion sensors or the Advertising Identifier.

Network use

The entire question bank is bundled inside the app, so the app works fully offline. Your device contacts the network only in these cases:

  • Apple's App Store, when you buy, restore or manage a subscription, or when the app loads current prices. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. The developer never receives your payment details, your Apple Account credentials or your Apple Account email. Apple's handling of that transaction is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.
  • An external website you deliberately tap, such as a citation link to a regulator or a statute, the Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use, the support page, or a mailto: link. Those open in your browser or mail app, and from that point the destination site's own policies apply. The developer receives nothing about which links you tap.

The app has no server, no API, no login endpoint and no telemetry. There is nothing for it to call home to.

Children

The app is a professional licensing study tool intended for adults preparing for a provincial mortgage licence. It is not directed at children, contains no user-generated content, no messaging, no social features and no advertising, and it knowingly collects no information from anyone of any age.

Your rights

Data-protection laws such as Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection over personal information a business holds about you.

Because the developer holds no personal information about you at all, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on the developer's side. The study data described above is under your sole control on your own device, and Reset Progress deletes it immediately. If you would like written confirmation of that position, email dmjks120@gmail.com and you will receive a reply.

The app performs no sale or sharing of personal information as those terms are used in the California Consumer Privacy Act, and no cross-context behavioural advertising.
